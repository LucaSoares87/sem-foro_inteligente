## Metrics and gates
- One pending frame per source; new frames overwrite stale pending ones. Assert offered = overwritten + processed after bounded drain, and stage counts agree. Count completed within the original 12s offered window separately; do not hide drain latency or skipped samples.
- Completion age measured from scheduled arrival to completion of queue/optimization services; includes event-loop publication lag, waiting, subprocess IPC and processing. Record P95 and inference mean. Record per-source effective completed FPS, losses and min/max completion ratio. Sample worker CPU seconds and RSS from Linux /proc; these omit parent memory and are not whole-system resource totals.
- Capacity gate for each source: at least 95% of offered frames completed within original window AND completion-age P95 <= 1000/offered FPS ms. Failures are recorded as experimental results, not hidden; CI success means accounting and safeguards pass, not necessarily that the capacity gate passes.
