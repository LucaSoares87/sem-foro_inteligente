## Candidate fixed before new simulations
Assume independent Bernoulli opportunities at one-second ticks. Stress arrival rates by +0.05 absolute, service rate by -0.05, clip to [0,1]. These margins are engineering hypotheses, not confidence intervals, empirically calibrated bounds or legal signal parameters. Enumerate exact discrete probability distributions by convolution, not by peeking at realized arrivals. For each hypothetical first window t=1..30, with frozen 4s transition, compute probability major arrivals exceed floor(0.85*capacity)-initial major queue before service. For minor compute distribution of cumulative net service opportunities minus arrivals over t; net>=initial queue is sufficient for clearance at least once, not for being empty at t. X(t) is largest integer initial minor queue with at least 95% probability of this sufficient condition (capped at floor(0.85*minor capacity)), or zero if major risk >5% or no feasible count. Zero is not an automatic permission: explicit probability checks required. X_robust is maximum X(t); select earliest window with both probability conditions and actual minor queue <=X(t). Individual 5% assumptions imply a union bound up to 10% for either failure under the assumed model, not a real-world guarantee.
Only change original minor-first preference: retain with earliest eligible experimental window, otherwise veto to original major-first plan as explicit synthetic comparator. Original non-minor preferences and low-confidence abstention preserved. A veto does not certify major-first as safe; all outcomes remain operational abstention. No control path. This changes order and possibly window length, so compare joint strategy, not isolated order effect. No tuning after seeing holdouts.
