## Study design (before execution)
Use actual frozen optimization evaluate() on synthetic QueueSnapshot inputs with known initial conditions and assumed arrival/discharge means. Confidence 0.95 is a synthetic scenario input, not a camera calibration or promotion. Separate negative case 0.64 must abstain. All outputs remain synthetic, simulation only, operational_decision=abstain. No tuning of production weights or thresholds.

Independent integer FIFO simulator, not production _simulate or its weighted cost. Eight fixed scenario families: light asymmetric, balanced, dominant growth, near storage, feasible near boundary, minor without net clearance, burst uncertainty and empty. Forty fixed seeds per family (100..139), alternate group labels by seed. 320 scenarios × 5 policy variants = 1600 counterfactual executions. Identical exogenous arrivals and service opportunities within each scenario; independent Bernoulli draws by group/tick, burst family uses alternating 1.8/0.2 arrival probability multipliers. Arrival before discharge each tick. Initial waiting age zero. Conservation checked. No physical blocking, turning, pedestrian movements, detector noise, lane geometry, demand calibration or downstream interaction modeled. Overcapacity remains in queue as exposure, not a physically realistic spillback model.

Compare fixed A-first and fixed B-first (28s each green opportunity within 60s horizon with one 4s transition), minor-first and major-first using equal first windows from production simulation (ceil for integer ticks, fallback 28s when no candidate metrics), and dynamic_x selection of one of those orderings. If no material advantage, synthetic comparator uses fixed A as explicit fallback, not an operational recommendation. Fixed A and B results reported separately; no oracle picks best. Different first windows make fixed-vs-dynamic comparison a joint timing/order comparison, not an isolated order effect. No recurring cycles or real controller timings.

Metrics: queue area, peak, departures, remaining, maximum observed waiting including right-censored queued vehicles, exposure above 85% storage and physical capacity, whether second group exceeds 85% before service. Summarize by family and overall, paired queue-area wins/losses/ties against major-first. Count cases where mean-rate X declares minor feasible yet simulated major crosses critical storage before its service, separately count actually chosen minor cases. This is a stress diagnostic, not a probability estimate for actual traffic. Do not require favorable results for CI success. No field accuracy inference, no general superiority from synthetic results. Full raw synthetic case metrics and deterministic report hash retained.
