## Fixed protocol before execution
16 families: 8 prior families with fresh seeds, 8 novel families (including demand regime shifts, service interruptions and bursts). Seeds 3000..3019, disjoint from SI-11A/B; 160 cases per cohort, 320 total, 6 policies = 1920 simulated trajectories, four consecutive 60-tick cycles each (7680 simulated cycles). Exogenous arrivals and service opportunities identical per case across all policies. Randomness shared within case, not independent across every family. Alternate group labels. Realized future draws unavailable to policy. Exact simulated queue and wait age at each cycle boundary; nominal rates known as synthetic inputs, not sensor estimates. Arrival/service changes deliberately violate forecast assumptions. FIFO vehicle conservation; no downstream blocking, turns, pedestrian phases or field calibration.

Each policy receives precisely 52 service opportunity ticks and 8 transition ticks per cycle, two nonoverlapping group service windows of 12..40 ticks each. Second window = 52 minus first. A terminal transition always charged, even if next first group equals previous second; this artificial common budget ensures comparable evaluation, not physical timing guidance. All policies re-evaluated at each cycle boundary. Original X and Robust X use frozen evaluate()/choose() decisions, with their selected first windows clamped to shared 12..40 range; no-material-advantage uses alternating equal allocation for all such cases. These are explicitly adapted comparators, not direct reproduction of SI-11A/B one-cycle results. Fixed alternating, demand-proportional (queue + expected arrivals divided by discharge rate), adapted original X, adapted Robust X, predictive X and predictive without X (ablation) all evaluated. Same realized demand; no policy gets extra green time.

Candidate: finite beam search over first group and 12/20/26/32/40 tick split options, 2-cycle planning horizon, replan after executing only the first simulated cycle. Fluid forecast with nominal and stressed (+0.05 arrivals/-0.05 service) rates, equally weighted. Score = queue area + 4*critical exposure + 15*overflow exposure + normalized occupancy imbalance; terminal queue penalty 6 per vehicle. Top 3 first-step plans retained. Joint second-step action must be allowed in both forecast states. For a minor-first plan require prior SI-11B probability gate at its selected first window; major-first options have independent split optimization, but no guarantee of safety. Risk profile is an assumed distribution, not calibrated. No feasible common next action => explicit abstention; an alternating equal allocation is used only to continue synthetic comparator accounting, recorded as fallback and fails the scientific gate. Low confidence always abstains. The ablation removes the X constraint but not other restrictions. No operational configuration or actuator.

## New research gates (do not replace SI-11A/B)
For each cohort and each of four baselines (adapted original X, adapted Robust X, demand-proportional, fixed alternating): mean queue area <=105% baseline, departures >=95%, critical and overflow exposure <= baseline, mean maximum observed waiting <=105% baseline. In addition, no predictive fallback and strict improvement in queue area or critical exposure vs adapted original X. Report all failures, family means and the ablation. These are point estimates, not confidence intervals or operational acceptance. No tuning on the new cohorts. CI verifies scientific integrity, not success of the candidate. No automatic promotion even if every gate passes. Previous SI-11B minor-choice gates retained in their own experiment; not directly comparable to this changed multi-cycle protocol.
