## Hypothesis registered before SI-09E replay
The existing counting-anchor limit combines five frames and 0.75s; at 30 FPS the frame limit is ~0.167s, at 15 ~0.333s, at 10 ~0.5s, at 5 the time limit dominates. Changing this experimental geometry field only to ceil(0.75*FPS), capped at 30, may reduce cadence-dependent missed crossings. It does not change the tracker's association, recover lost identities, prevent all duplicate counts or prove accuracy. Both variants consume exactly the same TrackBatch at each rate. One inference per native frame, deterministic decimation, no public boxes or trajectories. Compare line/direction counts and queue estimates separately; no candidate promoted just for agreeing with 30 FPS. Cache latency is per-frame inference cost, not measured live throughput. Track IDs are not unique physical vehicles.

## Independent reference
Manual visual annotations without model predictions; a separate reviewer must adjudicate counts and verify all 64 queue labels (two approaches × 32 seconds). Record explicit zero only after inspection; absent values remain unknown. Queue labels are at the last native frame in each second, comparisons to lower rate last samples are approximate (max alignment offset <0.2s). Annotate vehicles stopped for at least one second within the existing rectangular ROI using image-foot convention, flag ambiguous occlusion and leave unresolved labels absent. Count both directions for both finite line segments, not whole intersection throughput. Use original 960-frame span defined in preflight, not source frame numbering. Keep annotator references non-identifying; no plates/faces, frames or raw clips in Git. A reviewed reference requires exact video and geometry hashes, separate reviewer, explicit coverage and no missing keys. Review remains external; tool cannot certify that reviewer is genuine. No synthetic or detector-generated truth. Identity-level truth, absolute physical capacity and signal phase confirmation remain separate gates.

Research thresholds proposed before independent evaluation: directional count absolute error / total truth <= 5% when total >0; if total=0 require zero absolute error and report relative error undefined. Queue MAE <=1 vehicle; minimum confidence per eligible group >=0.65 only after actual calibration. No promotion until separate holdout, direction/phase/capacity review, X and agreement gates in frozen v1. No modification of SI-08B or operational thresholds.
